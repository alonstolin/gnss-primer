# GNSS Primer

A step-by-step walk through GPS on one real day's sky (4 April 2013, near Barcelona): from the broadcast
ephemeris file to the I/Q samples of a software-defined radio, through a receiver to the position and the
clock error, then one jammer and one spoofer. Interactive, in one HTML file.

Open it: https://alonstolin.github.io/gnss-primer/

Part 0 (the samples and the two files) is written. Parts 1 and 2 are in work.

Data: the broadcast ephemeris `brdc0940.13n` (International GNSS Service) and a YUMA almanac of GPS week
1734 (CelesTrak), both public.
